# TickSynchronizer golden vectors

All vectors are protocol bytes, not host-native memory dumps.

bit_fields_3_5.bin
  Operations: write_bits(0b101, 3), write_bits(0b11011, 5)
  Hex: DD

unaligned_1_8.bin
  Operations: write_bits(1, 1), write_bits(0xA5, 8)
  Hex: 4B 01

uint64_little_endian.bin
  Operation: write_bits(0x0123456789ABCDEF, 64)
  Hex: EF CD AB 89 67 45 23 01

fixed_unsigned_little_endian.bin
  Operations: write_u8(0x5A), write_u16(0x1234),
              write_u32(0x12345678), write_u64(0x0123456789ABCDEF)
  Hex: 5A 34 12 78 56 34 12 EF CD AB 89 67 45 23 01

varuint_boundaries.bin
  Values: 0, 1, 127, 128, 255, 300, 16383, 16384,
          UINT32_MAX, UINT64_MAX
  Hex: 00 01 7F 80 01 FF 01 AC 02 FF 7F 80 80 01 FF FF FF FF 0F FF FF FF FF FF FF FF FF FF 01

zigzag_boundaries.bin
  Values: 0, -1, 1, -2, 2, -1000, 1000, INT64_MIN, INT64_MAX
  Hex: 00 01 02 03 04 CF 0F D0 0F FF FF FF FF FF FF FF FF FF 01 FE FF FF FF FF FF FF FF FF 01

float32_ieee754.bin
  00000000 80000000 3F800000 C0200000 7F800000 FF800000 7FC00001 00000001
  IEEE 754 binary32 patterns encoded little-endian: +0, -0, 1, -2.5, +inf, -inf, quiet NaN payload, minimum subnormal.

float64_ieee754.bin
  0000000000000000 8000000000000000 3FF0000000000000 C004000000000000
  7FF0000000000000 FFF0000000000000 7FF8000000000001 0000000000000001
  IEEE 754 binary64 patterns encoded little-endian: +0, -0, 1, -2.5, +inf, -inf, quiet NaN payload, minimum subnormal.

control_hello_v1.bin
  Legacy control header + minimal HELLO payload from protocol 1.0.
  Retained to verify strict rejection and safe header inspection.
  Size: 52 bytes.

control_hello_v2.bin
  Current protocol 1.1 control header + identity-aware HELLO payload version 2.
  Includes exact Godot/module revisions, game/schema compatibility IDs,
  supported/required capabilities and nonce 0x8877665544332211.
  Size: 140 bytes.
